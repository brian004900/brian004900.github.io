Featured side research

R²TC-MIL: keep every patch. Change what the model listens to.

Whole-slide pathology images are enormous, while labels often exist only at the slide level. My R²TC-MIL project adds a lightweight Cluster-Conditioned Instance Reweighting step to refine which instances matter during MIL aggregation.

R²TC-MIL / SLN-BREASTweakly supervised WSI classification
Open repository ↗
CORE IDEA

Cluster context becomes a soft signal, not a hard filter.

Instead of selecting or dropping patches, CIR scores bag-wise clusters and maps those scores back into soft instance weights. Every patch stays in the bag; its influence changes.
